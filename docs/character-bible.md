Reviewed on 2026-09-06 against the four cached `data.content` excerpts and all
16 characters declared in `content/worlds.ts` and `content/future-island.ts`.
This covers the authored cast, not every person mentioned by the sources.
Exact filenames, scene IDs and remaining work are in
[cast-production-plan.md](art/cast-production-plan.md).

Fang Nuo's existing root-reviewed integrated-v3 main/reaction pair is fixed and
remains the current production identity for user review, not a claim of user

current accepted generated sprites. Zhang Wei's previous attempt returned HTTP

## Evidence And Drawing Authority

- **Source** means directly supported by the local excerpt. **Game** means an
  existing authored adaptation choice. **Design** means original visual choices
  fixed here. Specific ages, anatomy, hair, costume and accessories are not
  source facts unless explicitly identified as such.
- All 16 designs are unambiguously adult. Existing game ages take precedence.
  The observer's 42 and operator's 35 are design ages; their game labels remain
  simply adult. Future Island classmates are graduated adults, not pupils.
- `docs/references/user-character-full-crop.png` and
  `docs/references/user-character-face.png` control character drawing: mature
  late-1990s Japanese animation anatomy, variable cleanup lines, grouped hair,
  narrow readable eyes, opaque colors and restrained expression. Transfer the
  drawing language, not the reference person's identity. Screenshot text still
  visible in the crop is unrelated and must not appear in art.
- The gothic reference controls separate painted backgrounds. Characters need
  anatomically placed hard shadows at brow/socket, nose side/cast shadow,
  cheek turn, lower lip/chin and jaw-to-neck. Use one connected main shadow and
  at most one overlap shadow, with a readable lit eye and sclera. Darkness,
  airbrush or glossy skin cannot substitute for structure. Full authority:
  [art-direction.md](art-direction.md).
- Every numbered anchor below is fixed across main/reaction. Default is the
  same body, hands, props, hair, costume, framing and light, changing only
  brows, lids and mouth. A separately planned shoulder/neck change cannot alter
  the skeleton or conceal an anchor. Reaction must derive from the accepted
  actual main, not another text-only interpretation of the same person.
- At least three bone-matrix columns must remain visibly different between
  each pair of cast members. Hair and accessories do not count toward that
  requirement. Behavior conveys harm; a diagnosis-coded face, deformity or
  sexualized threat is not characterization.
- Ordinary covered winter silhouettes use matte black/charcoal, wine red,
  cool gray and small old-silver fittings. The observer must retain visibly
  gray outerwear even where that changes the default black percentage. A tiny
  dull-gold story coin is allowed. No armor, tactical costume, school uniform,
  excessive decoration or fashion-model proportions. Gothic ornament belongs
  to the setting, not to everyone's clothing.
